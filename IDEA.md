## Peptide Connect

Peptide Connect is an evidence-first supplier intelligence and verification platform for the fragmented research-peptide market.

The market's useful information is scattered across independent laboratory reports, supplier COAs, vendor websites, B2B directories, WhatsApp and Telegram conversations, certification documents, regulatory records, and community testing databases. Supplier identities are often inconsistent, test coverage varies widely, and marketing claims are frequently presented alongside analytical evidence without a clear distinction between what is claimed, independently verified, or still unknown.

Peptide Connect turns that fragmented information into structured, provenance-backed supplier dossiers.

The platform normalizes supplier identities and aliases, links analytical reports to compounds and lots where possible, distinguishes vendor-submitted testing from independently sourced evidence, measures analytical test coverage, records supplier claims separately from verified facts, surfaces contradictions and missing evidence, and preserves the source behind every material assertion.

Rather than declaring a supplier "good," "bad," or "safe," Peptide Connect shows users what the available evidence actually establishes and where uncertainty remains.

The initial MVP focuses on ingesting and normalizing public analytical records and supplier information into a searchable evidence database. Later phases may add automated supplier due-diligence workflows, monitored WhatsApp/Telegram research communications, change detection, community-funded independent testing, Peptide Connect-controlled mystery purchases sent to third-party laboratories, longitudinal quality analysis, supplier transparency metrics, alerts, and B2B/API access.

The long-term goal is to become the intelligence layer between fragmented peptide-market data and the people trying to understand it: a continuously updated system for supplier identity, analytical evidence, provenance, testing coverage, transparency, and historical consistency.
